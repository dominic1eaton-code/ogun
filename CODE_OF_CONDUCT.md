# Code of Conduct

ogun OS is a technical project with ambitious systems work and many sharp edges.
We want the community around it to be serious, curious, direct, and humane.
This code of conduct applies to project repositories, issues, merge requests,
discussions, chats, events, documentation spaces, and any other official project
venue.

## Our Standards

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful of different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and
  communication styles.
- Critique ideas, code, architecture, and tradeoffs without attacking people.
- Make technical claims with evidence when possible.
- Assume honest mistakes first, especially in an early-stage codebase.
- Accept maintainer decisions even when you disagree.
- Keep security-sensitive information private until disclosure is coordinated.
- Help make discussions clear for future readers.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking, threats, or sustained disruption.
- Discriminatory language or conduct related to race, ethnicity, nationality,
  caste, religion, disability, neurotype, age, sex, gender identity, sexual
  orientation, veteran status, body size, or other protected or personal
  characteristics.
- Sexualized language, imagery, attention, or advances in project spaces.
- Publishing private communications or personal information without consent.
- Mocking, humiliating, or dogpiling contributors.
- Deliberately derailing issues, reviews, or support channels.
- Publicly disclosing vulnerabilities before a reasonable private disclosure
  process has occurred.

## Technical Disagreement

Strong technical disagreement is allowed. The standard is how it is handled.

Good disagreement:

- Names the concrete risk or tradeoff.
- Points to code, docs, tests, specs, traces, or reproducible behavior.
- Offers a path forward.
- Leaves room for maintainers to make a decision.

Bad disagreement:

- Questions a person's intelligence, motives, identity, or belonging.
- Repeats the same point after a decision has been made.
- Turns review into a loyalty test or status contest.

## Reporting

To report conduct concerns, contact the project steward listed in
`GOVERNANCE.md` or use the private reporting channel provided by the project
host if available.

Include:

- What happened.
- Where it happened.
- Who was involved.
- Links, screenshots, or timestamps when available.
- Whether there is an immediate safety concern.

Reports will be handled with as much confidentiality as practical. Retaliation
against someone for making a good-faith report is not allowed.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take actions including:

- A private clarification or warning.
- Temporary moderation of a thread.
- Removal of comments or content.
- Closing or locking an issue or merge request.
- Temporary or permanent loss of project participation privileges.
- Escalation to the hosting platform for severe cases.

Enforcement decisions consider impact, severity, history, and whether the
behavior stopped after being addressed.

## Scope

This code applies in official project spaces and when someone is representing
the project in public. It may also apply outside official spaces when conduct
creates a safety risk or materially harms participation in the project.
